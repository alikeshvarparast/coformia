Security

How this website and our product conversations handle data.

Website

coformia.com is hosted on infrastructure we operate in Canada, with Cloudflare providing DNS, TLS at the edge, and caching. Mail from the contact form goes through our mail provider (Resend or the SMTP service configured for the site).

Customer data

Formulane and Qualane pilots use data you provide under a project agreement. That data is not used to train public AI models. People on your team approve releases; an assistant only drafts when asked.
