Human-in-the-loop AI for batch planning and allergen checks

The useful question is not “Can AI plan production?” It is which steps save time without hiding accountability when something goes wrong.

Good fits for AI

Scaling a approved formula to three batch sizes, summing ingredient totals, flagging stock shortfalls, and drafting purchase requests are repetitive, data-heavy tasks. They are also where manual spreadsheet edits cause the most silent errors.

Poor fits for full automation

Allergen declarations, customer-specific exceptions, and “can we substitute ingredient X this week?” are judgment calls. They need a named approver, not a model confidence score displayed to nobody.

How Formulane applies the split

In Formulane, the assistant prepares batch calculations and documents. QA and operations approve before release. The audit trail shows what the model proposed and what humans accepted—relevant for specification change control and customer audits.

Private by design

Canadian hosting and private AI that does not train on your formulas are table stakes for manufacturers evaluating vendors. Always confirm data handling in your pilot contract.

FAQ

What should AI do in batch planning?

Read inputs, scale quantities, check stock against requirements, and draft documents for review—not release production without approval.

What must stay human?

Allergen risk judgments, customer-specific commitments, and any release to ERP or the plant floor.
