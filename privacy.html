Privacy

This page describes what coformia.com collects. Coformia is a software company in Toronto, Canada.

Contact form

If you send the form, we receive your name, email address, and message. A Cloudflare Turnstile check confirms the submission is from a person. We email that message to our team (via Resend or SMTP). We do not sell this information.

Analytics

We use Plausible for aggregate page counts. The script does not load when the browser sends Do Not Track. Plausible is cookieless.

Hosting

The site is served from our own infrastructure, with Cloudflare in front for DNS and caching.

Deletion

To ask us to delete a message you sent, email info@coformia.com.
