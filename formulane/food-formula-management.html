Food formula management software for real plants

Formulane gives food manufacturers one connected record for customer formulas, specifications, changes, and approvals—without replacing the ERP your operations team already runs.

When spreadsheets become the system of record

Contract manufacturers and branded producers often juggle dozens of customer formulas. Each customer sends updates by email. R&D keeps a master sheet. Production prints batch documents from another folder. QA signs allergen checks in a third tool. By the time a change reaches the ERP, someone has already run a batch on the old numbers.

Food formula management software should not add another silo. It should be the live bridge between how your teams actually work and the BOMs your ERP expects.

What Formulane keeps together

  • Customer-owned formulas and specifications, separated by customer and SKU
  • Change requests with a clear approval path—QA, customer sign-off, operations
  • Batch calculations from one tested engine, not hand-edited cells
  • Released BOMs and batch sheets only after people approve

AI prepares the work. People approve.

Formulane uses AI to read files, fill forms, and run the calculations your specialists do by hand today. Nothing is released automatically. Every step is on record, which matters for audits, customer disputes, and allergen traceability.

Connected to ERP, not a replacement

Formulane is built to work with SAP and other ERPs: materials, prices, and stock flow in; approved formulas and BOMs flow out after confirmation. See our ERP integration approach for detail.

Common questions

What is food formula management software?

Software that keeps each customer formula, specification, and approved change in one live record instead of scattered spreadsheets and email threads.

Does Formulane replace our ERP?

No. Formulane works with SAP and other ERPs: it reads materials and stock, and releases approved BOMs after your team signs off.
