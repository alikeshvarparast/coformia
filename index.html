Questions we hear first.

What does Coformia actually build?

Connected software for work that still lives in spreadsheets, inboxes, and chats. AI prepares the work. A person approves it.

Do we have to replace our ERP?

No. The goal is one live record that stays in sync with the systems you already run, including ERP.

What is Formulane?

Formula, specification, change, and approval software for food manufacturers. Change once, and the connected record stays right everywhere.

What is CountIn?

Community and group activity management: who is coming, who is waiting, and who owes what. CountIn does not take the payment.

How does a project start?

Understand how the work moves, prototype with your data, pilot with one team, then scale only if the pilot holds.

How do we reach you?
