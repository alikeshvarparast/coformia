Questions we hear first.

Short answers to what most teams ask before a first call. Ask us anything else.

What does Coformia build?

Connected apps for work that still runs on paper, spreadsheets and email chains. Your team creates, reviews and approves in one place, and an AI assistant helps when you ask.

Do we have to replace our ERP?

No. We keep one live record in sync with the systems you already run, including your ERP.

Can you build software just for us?

Yes. From a single tool to a full platform for production, quality or supply chain, built around how your team works.

How does a project start?

We map one process, show you a prototype with your data, pilot it with one team, and scale only if it works.
