Questions we hear first.

What organisers ask us most about CountIn. Ask us anything else.

What does CountIn track?

Who is coming, who is waiting, and who owes what. Members mark going or not going, the waitlist fills open spots, and a shared ledger tracks the split.

Does CountIn take the payment?

No. CountIn never takes the payment. Cash and e-transfer stay between members.

Where is CountIn?

CountIn is live at https://countin.sspi.trade/.

Who is CountIn for?

Groups that meet on purpose: sports, clubs and community activities, with one organiser and repeating or one-off sessions.

Can a group be private?

Yes. A public group can be found by name and joined on request. A private group is joined only with an invite link.
