About Coformia

We turn work that still runs on paper, spreadsheets, and group chats into connected digital processes—and use AI where it makes those processes faster, more accurate, and more reliable.

Coformia is a software company in Toronto, Canada. This website, coformia.com, is ours. We build Formulane, Qualane and CountIn.

What we build

Coformia is the company behind Formulane, formulation management for food manufacturers, Qualane, quality control for food and beverage plants, and CountIn, community and group activity management. We co-founded ShiftiAI for fair shift planning. ShiftiAI is a separate product, not another name for Coformia.

We also build custom software and AI for a company's own processes. The rule is always the same: people decide, and the AI assistant helps when asked.

How we work with customers

We map how work really moves, prototype with your data, pilot with one team, and scale only when the pilot works. No ERP replacement: one live record that stays in sync with the systems you already run.
