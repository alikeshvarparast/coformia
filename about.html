About Coformia

We turn work that still runs on paper, spreadsheets, and group chats into connected digital processes—and use AI where it makes those processes faster, more accurate, and more reliable.

What we build

Coformia is the company behind Formulane, formula and specification management for food manufacturers, Qualane, shift-level quality control for food plants, and CountIn, community and group activity management. We co-founded ShiftiAI for fair shift planning.

Across products, the contract is the same: AI prepares the work. People approve. Software should not silently override human judgment on regulated or customer-facing decisions.

How we work with customers

We map how work really moves, prototype with your data, pilot with one team, and scale only when the pilot proves value. No big-bang ERP replacements—one live record that stays in sync with systems you already run.

Founder

Ali Keshvarparast founded Coformia and created CountIn. He co-founded ShiftiAI. He works directly with early Formulane customers on formula workflow and ERP integration.
